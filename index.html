— How We Work

An unhurried process.

i.

Consultation

Send a photo or stop by the workshop. We'll talk through the piece, the use, and the look you're after.

ii.

Fabric & Quote

Choose from our sample library or bring your own. A written quote follows within a few days.

iii.

Workshop

Your piece is stripped, rebuilt where needed, and rewrapped by hand. Typical timeline: 3–6 weeks.

iv.

Delivery

White-glove pickup and delivery throughout Tallahassee and the surrounding region.
